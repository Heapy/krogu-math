## Finite arithmetic proof of the classifier

Let `S=sa.toLong()-sb`. Its full range is[-4294967295,4294967295]. Because the
coefficient is1 and b is at most3, the following exhausts this grid.

For b=1 the natural exact pair is `1@S`. For b=2 it is `5@(S+1)`: multiply
1/2 by10 to obtain5. Each coefficient has one digit, no trailing zero and
fits all five positive precisions. In-range natural scales therefore require
these exact pairs, independent of mode. A scale above MAX has no integral
coefficient at a smaller supported scale, since neither1 nor5 is divisible by10.
Below MIN, an equivalent pair can sometimes be formed by adding coefficient
zeros; the existing560 policy keeps these LOW cases admitted rather than
mandating padding. The character model returns `!AdmittedScale` precisely when
the digits needed for that alternative do not exceed p; if even that minimum
digit count exceeds p, no precision-respecting supported cohort exists and it
returns the required AE tag. The candidate does not clamp an endpoint or alter
these cohort rules.

For b=3, the quotient is nonterminating. UNNECESSARY requires AE independently
of which scale/range failure another algorithm would encounter first. For a
rounding mode allowing inexactness and p=1,2,7, the normalized coefficient is
p threes for DOWN/FLOOR/each HALF mode, or its single increment ending in four
for UP/CEILING. All operands are positive. The first discarded digit is3,
strictly below half, so no tie/carry ambiguity exists here. The coefficient has
exactly p digits and no trailing zero; the selected scale is `S+p`. An in-range
scale requires that exact coefficient/scale/text, otherwise the selected rounded
cohort has no supported scale. This reasoning is independent of a normalization
numerator and does not accept an AE when a supported result is required.

For b=3 and p=MAX-1/MAX, any permitted inexact rounded coefficient still has p
digits. Even the lower bound `10^(p-1) > 2^(3(p-1))` exceeds the supported
BigInteger bit ceiling because3(p-1)>MAX. Thus this is a necessary unsupported
final coefficient, not rejection because a dispensable intermediate was large.
UNNECESSARY independently requires AE. No enormous coefficient is constructed
by the expected model.

These cases match the existing character model's branches: denominators1/2
cancel to exact one-digit results; denominator3 has no cancellation and reaches
the `p>256` error tag for the two huge precisions. Only p=1,2,7 reach its
`repeat(p-1)` rounding path. Its exact LOW digit test is `1+MIN-E`, with
E=S or S+1, computed in Long. The largest scale sum here is
4294967295+2147483647=6442450942, less than2^33. The smallest relevant S and
all exact-place adjustments also fit Long. Character arithmetic only processes
the tiny coefficients/denominators and at most an eight-digit scaled numerator;
small digit products/carries are bounded by99. The model has no allocation
proportional to MAX precision in this grid.

The proof is deliberately limited to positive coefficients1/1,1/2,1/3, these
finite scales/precisions and these contexts. It does not weaken signed/tie,
subtype, callback, identity, other division-family, null or range assertions
elsewhere. Their existing tests and the seven frozen581 files remain unchanged.

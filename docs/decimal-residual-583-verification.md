## Public contract assessment and intentional divergence

and chooses exact cohorts closest to preferred scale within precision.
[Integral division with context](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/25/docs/api/java.base/java/math/BigDecimal.html#divideToIntegralValue(java.math.BigDecimal,java.math.MathContext))
requires the integer part independently of rounding mode, rejecting zero divisors
or insufficient result precision.
[Remainder](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/25/docs/api/java.base/java/math/BigDecimal.html#remainder(java.math.BigDecimal,java.math.MathContext))
is exact, with context affecting implicit division;
[pair](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/25/docs/api/java.base/java/math/BigDecimal.html#divideAndRemainder(java.math.BigDecimal,java.math.MathContext))
returns the corresponding quotient/remainder. These are paraphrased public rules,
not implementation-source observations.
